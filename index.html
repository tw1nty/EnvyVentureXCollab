This Session

Core Pressure, Pelvic Floor & True Stability

This session will take a deeper, more intentional approach to movement by focusing on intra-abdominal pressure (IAP) — specifically how we can use it to re-engage the pelvic floor and better connect with the transverse abdominals.

From there, we'll show members how this directly improves posture, stability, and overall balance in the body.

About Virgil Keen

Virgil Keen is a certified personal trainer and movement specialist dedicated to helping individuals restore strength, eliminate pain, and build lasting performance through intelligent training systems.

With a background rooted in the medical field, Virgil developed an early understanding of anatomy, rehabilitation principles, and the interconnected relationship between the muscular, neurological, and skeletal systems. His exposure to clinical environments deepened his respect for precision, assessment, and individualized care — principles that now form the foundation of his coaching philosophy.

Rather than chasing trends, Virgil focuses on movement quality first. His work is heavily influenced by Dynamic Neuromuscular Stabilization (DNS), an advanced rehabilitation approach that retrains the body through developmental movement patterns. Currently pursuing advanced DNS certification, he integrates these principles into strength training, mobility restoration, and performance programming.

Through ENVY Fitness, Virgil focuses on:
• Postural restoration
• Pain-free strength development
• Corrective mobility systems
• Functional performance enhancement
"My mission is simple: To help people move better, feel stronger, and reclaim confidence in their bodies."

Based in Frederick, Maryland, ENVY Fitness offers personalized training, small-group coaching, mobility programs, and online coaching designed to bridge the gap between rehabilitation and performance. Virgil's long-term vision is to expand ENVY Fitness into a premier movement facility that blends clinical precision with elite-level training — creating a space where longevity, strength, and resilience are built the right way.
